Usage Instructions

  1. Navigate to any webpage with Japanese text
  2. Hover your mouse over a Japanese sentence
  3. Press Ctrl + Alt together
  4. A popup will appear showing the grammar patterns detected

About Bunpochan

Version: 0.2.0 (MVP)

Bunpochan is a Japanese grammar learning assistant that helps you understand grammar patterns while reading. Similar to Yomitan for vocabulary, bunpochan provides on-demand grammar explanations.

Current database includes 125+ grammar patterns covering all JLPT levels (N5-N1).
